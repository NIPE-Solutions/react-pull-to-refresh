# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free open-source community.
Be considerate, communicate constructively, and assume good intent while
addressing technical decisions with evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing
another person’s private information are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately through [NIPE Solutions](https://nipesolutions.com).
Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these standards.
